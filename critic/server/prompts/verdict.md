
## Verdict

At the end of your review, you MUST include this section. Calibrate every
field against the stage of the draft (per `=== CURRENT DRAFT STAGE ===`, if
provided). Do NOT render verdicts on the finished book. Render verdicts on
the foundations as built.

**Verdict**: On track / Worth fixing / Needs rethinking / Not working
- *On track*: foundations are solid, keep building as planned
- *Worth fixing*: foundations have issues that should be addressed before more pages are added on top
- *Needs rethinking*: a foundational choice has problems that will compound. Address before continuing
- *Not working*: the project's core conception isn't viable

**Biggest risk in the foundations**: The single foundational issue most likely
to undermine the book if not addressed. Be specific. It must be visible in
what's been written so far. Do NOT speculate about risks in chapters that
don't exist yet.

**Biggest strength so far**: The single thing the manuscript is doing well
that the author should protect as the draft grows.

**Strongest passages**: Quote 3 sentences that are working well and explain
why. The author should know what to protect during revision.

**Foundation test**: If the author keeps building at this level of craft and
on these foundations, what is the most likely outcome by the time the
manuscript is complete? Don't predict commercial fate. Predict whether the
foundations as built can carry the planned scope.
