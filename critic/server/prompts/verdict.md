
## Verdict

At the end of your review, you MUST include this section:

**Verdict**: On track / Worth fixing / Needs rethinking / Not working
- *On track*: strong, keep going as planned
- *Worth fixing*: has issues but they're addressable in revision
- *Needs rethinking*: the foundation has problems that should be addressed before more pages are written
- *Not working*: the project itself isn't viable

**Biggest commercial risk**: The single thing most likely to make a publisher pass on the finished book.
**Biggest commercial strength**: The single thing most likely to make a publisher say yes.
**Strongest passages**: Quote 3 sentences that are working well and explain why. The author should know what to protect during revision.
**One-line pitch test**: If the agent had to pitch this to an editor in one sentence, what would they say? If you can't formulate one, say so and explain why — that's a finding.
